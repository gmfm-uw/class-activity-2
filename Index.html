Campus Study Space Guide

Finding a Good Place to Study

Students need different kinds of study spaces depending on the work they are doing.

Some spaces are designed for quiet individual study, while others are better for group collaboration.

What to Look For

  • Available seating
  • Power outlets
  • Reliable Wi-Fi
  • Low noise level

How to Choose a Study Space

  1. Decide whether you need individual or group space
  2. Check the expected noise level
  3. Check whether power outlets are available
  4. Select the space that best fits your needs
